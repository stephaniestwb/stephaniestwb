## 🌊 A Little About Me

🎓 I'm a **Master's student in Animal Sciences at The Ohio State University**, with an undergraduate background in Chemical Engineering.

🦠 My research focuses on **porcine rotavirus A and C**, particularly viral genomics, genetic diversity, and enteric versus respiratory infection.

🔬 I enjoy combining experimental virology with computational approaches to investigate biological questions.

💻 I'm especially interested in metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, molecular evolution, and developing reproducible bioinformatics workflows.

🌱 Currently expanding my knowledge of **molecular virology, computational biology, and machine learning**.

🎯 My goal is to become a researcher who can move comfortably between the laboratory and computational analysis.

---

## 🧪 What I'm Working On

- 🦠 **Rotavirus genomics:** Genomic characterization and genetic diversity of porcine RVA and RVC.
- 🧬 **Viral tropism:** Investigating rotavirus detection in paired nasal and rectal samples.
- 🦠 **Metagenomics:** Characterizing microbial communities and their functional potential.
- 💻 **Bioinformatics:** Building and improving reproducible workflows for biological data analysis.
- 📚 **Continuous learning:** Strengthening my knowledge of virology, statistics, and computational methods.
